## Overview
NAFATH is a public-service AI assistant designed to help people access information clearly and reliably. A trustworthy assistant must provide accurate, understandable answers while protecting users’ personal information. Its design should combine reliable software, responsible AI, secure mobile access, resilient networking, cybersecurity controls, and well-managed data. These six areas work together to improve service availability, privacy, security, and user confidence. When information is uncertain, the assistant should communicate its limitations rather than present guesses as facts.
